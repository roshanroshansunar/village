Culture and traditions

Life in Fulbari follows the festival calendar and the farming seasons.

Dashain

The biggest festival of the year. Families gather, elders put red tika and jamara on younger ones' foreheads, and kites fill the sky.

Tihar

Five days of lights, marigold garlands, deusi-bhailo songs and colourful rangoli at every doorstep.

Food

Dal bhat with gundruk, sel roti on festival mornings, dhido, sisnu soup, and fresh milk tea from the neighbours.

Farming life

Rice planting in monsoon, harvest songs in autumn, and a market day where the whole village meets.

Music and dance

Madal drums, folk songs and group dances at weddings, jatras and village gatherings.

Language and dress

Nepali is spoken daily. Daura suruwal and gunyo cholo are worn for celebrations.
